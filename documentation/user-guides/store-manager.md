# Dark store manager guide

* Pick your store in the **Site** selector at the top: every page then filters to it.
* **Fulfillment** shows accepted orders waiting to be picked (oldest first). Create single pick tasks,
  or tick several orders and choose *Create batch pick* for one combined route.
* **Ready for dispatch** → *Auto-assign rider* picks the least-busy available rider of your store.
* **Delivery**: register riders, set them available or offline, see live assignments and proofs.
* **Inventory → Stock**: per-SKU buckets. Click a row for batches in FEFO order; use *QC* to
  quarantine or damage units and *Adjust* to request corrections (a second person approves).
* **Counts**: *Start blind count* → staff enter counts without seeing system quantities → a
  different user reconciles. Shortfalls that would cut into customer reservations are reported for
  manual review instead of being booked.
* **Transfers** arriving from the central warehouse: *Receive all* or receive per line. Short
  receipts are written off and reported.
* **Store Sync**: if your store ran offline, conflicts (e.g. damage reported for stock already sold)
  appear here to *Retry* or *Dismiss*.

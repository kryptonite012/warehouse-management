# Customer guide

1. Enter your **PIN code**. You'll see products from your nearest store with live stock: if it says
   *Out of stock*, it really is.
2. Tap **ADD**, adjust quantities, open **Cart** → **Proceed to checkout**.
3. Choose or add a delivery address and a payment method. Cash on delivery is always available when the
   store enables it; online methods appear once the store has configured a payment gateway.
4. Your order page shows live status. When the rider is on the way you'll see a **4-digit delivery
   code**: share it only with the rider at your door.
5. You can cancel until the store starts picking, and request a return within 48 hours of delivery.
   Refunds are tracked on the order.

# Rider guide

Open `/rider` on your phone and sign in.

1. New deliveries appear in **My deliveries**. Open one and tap **Accept delivery**.
2. At the store, collect the labelled bag and tap **Picked up from store**.
3. **Open in maps** for directions. Your location is only recorded if your phone shares a real GPS fix
   when you tap a button. The app never invents positions.
4. At the door, ask the customer for their **4-digit delivery code** and enter it to confirm. For cash
   on delivery, collect the amount shown first.
5. If the delivery can't be completed, enter the reason and tap **Report failed delivery**. The parcel
   goes back to the store's dispatch queue.

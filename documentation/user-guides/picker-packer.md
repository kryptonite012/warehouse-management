# Picker & packer guide (mobile)

Open `/ops/pick` on the handheld. The first time, choose your store.

## Picking
1. Tap **Start picking** on the oldest order.
2. The screen shows the **bin to go to** in large type, the product photo, name, SKU, batch and expiry,
   and how many to take.
3. Scan the product barcode with the hardware scanner, or tap the camera button. A wrong product is
   rejected with a buzz. An expired batch is blocked: call your supervisor.
4. Lines are listed in walking order. When everything is scanned, tap **Complete & send to packing**.
   If an item is missing, use **Item not found — finish with short pick**: the unpicked units go
   straight back to stock.

## Packing
1. Switch to **Pack**, open the order and scan each item into the bag (or use +/−).
2. Choose the packaging (use the insulated bag for chilled items) and tap **Seal & label**.
   Quantities must match what was picked.
3. Print the label. COD orders show the amount to collect.
